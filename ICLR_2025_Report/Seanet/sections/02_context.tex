\section{Context of the Internship}
\label{sec:context}

\begin{guide}
Maximum one page, three short subsections. Use ``we/us'' for the team, ``I'' for
myself. Do not copy text from a website.
\textbf{Questions to answer:} Where did I work, and with whom? What does the team
work on, in one or two sentences? Why does \emph{this} team care about interpretable
time series models? What was my place in the team (desk, meetings, supervision,
compute access)?
\end{guide}

tracked (weekly meetings, slides, written reports, a shared repository)? Did I work
alone on the code, or with others?
