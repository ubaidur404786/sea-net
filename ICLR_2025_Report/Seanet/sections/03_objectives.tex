% \todo{Three or four sentences describing the path: baseline reproduction $\rightarrow$

{At the start, we found different papers to read, like the VQShape \citep{wen2024vqshape}. In their paper, they already train shapelets, and with the help of these shapelets, they explain the prediction. However, we wanted a simple and clear solution, not something too complex. Similarly, we used ideas from the MSDA MIL paper \citep{hashimoto2020msdamil}, like using multi-scale WSI images, and also the DSMIL \citep{li2021dsmil} dual-stream trick. The problem was that both of these work on WSI images to detect cancer using multiple instance learning, and their attention depends on their feature extractor, which did not work well in our domain. WSI images are 40k x 40k pixels and are converted into bags and patches, but our time series datasets have an average of 1000 to 1500 time points, so we have far fewer points. In this case, TCN methods are more suitable than their techniques. This is how our plan evolved, and we are still researching other papers to find the best pooling methods.}
