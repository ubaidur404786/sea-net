\begin{guide}
Three subsections: scientific, technical, professional. Two to four bullets each. For
every bullet, name the skill \emph{and} the moment in the project where I used it.
Write in the first person and in the past tense.
\textbf{Questions to answer:} What can I do now that I could not do in the first week?
Which mistake taught me the most? What would I do differently if I started this
project again tomorrow?
\end{guide}
