The host organization is the IRCICA / CRIStAL laboratory, and I worked with the FOX research team. They work on time series analysis across different domains. They also focus on hardware like microprocessors and Microcontroller Units (MCUs).

% \todo{One or two sentences on the team's research line that my subject belongs to

The title of my internship was "Time series classification using machine learning for edge devices (microcontrollers)." About 70\% of my work focused on time series classification using machine learning, and the remaining 30\% focused on edge devices (MCUs).

I met with my supervisor at least once a week, and we used Discord to communicate. We had a shared Git repository where he tracked my code. I wrote the code alone, but we discussed every idea first. I shared my ideas with my supervisor to get his guidance and feedback. We also read new research papers to exchange knowledge. After our discussions, I implemented the ideas and showed him the results.

For training the models on the full dataset, I used the Grid'5000 testbed \citep{balouek2013grid5000} for GPU training (using the Lille and Sophia sites). I used the OAR scheduler to submit my jobs.
